# Nominal vs Real Income: Why a Raise Can Buy Less

Audience: students and working adults. English; 55–75 seconds; clear and inquisitive.

Hook: Your paycheck grew. So why can you buy less?
Promise: distinguish the amount earned from purchasing power.
Payoff: +5% nominal, about -2.8% real.
Takeaway: nominal is what you earn; real is what it buys.
CTA: compare your raise with inflation over the same period.
