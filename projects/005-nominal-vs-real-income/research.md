# Nominal versus real income

Definitions and inflation adjustment verified against BLS and the St. Louis Fed. All amounts and rates in this video are hypothetical.

- nominal: Nominal income is money earned without adjusting for price changes.
- real: Real income adjusts nominal income for price changes to express purchasing power relative to a base period.
- prices: With fixed dollars, a higher general price level buys fewer goods and services.
- calculation: Hypothetical monthly income 3000 to 3150 is +5%; price index 100 to108 is +8%; 3150/1.08=2916.6667 base-year dollars; real growth=1.05/1.08-1=-2.7778%.

Sources:
- [Income and the Consumer Price Index (CPI)](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/factsheets/income.htm)
- [Adjusting for Inflation](https://www.stlouisfed.org/publications/page-one-economics/2023/01/03/adjusting-for-inflation)
