# Saturn: a surface that is not a surface

NASA Saturn Facts, https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/facts/ (accessed 2026-10-27).

- Saturn’s rings contain billions of small chunks of ice and rock, with dust and other material.
- Most ring particles range from dust-sized icy grains to house-sized chunks.
- Different rings orbit Saturn at different speeds.
