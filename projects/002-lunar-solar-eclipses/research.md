# Lunar and solar eclipses

Primary sources checked: NASA Science, 2026-10-05.

- For a solar eclipse the Moon is between Sun and Earth; its shadow reaches part of Earth.
- At total solar eclipse the bright solar disk is hidden and the corona can be seen.
- For a lunar eclipse Earth is between Sun and Moon; the Moon enters Earth's shadow.
- During total lunar eclipse sunlight filtered and bent by Earth's atmosphere can illuminate the Moon red/orange.
- The Moon's orbital plane is tilted about five degrees relative to Earth's orbital plane, so eclipses do not occur every month.
- Direct solar viewing needs appropriate solar protection; ordinary sunglasses are unsafe. Direct-view eclipse glasses should meet ISO 12312-2.
